## 1. Purpose

This pack prepares 365 to request legitimate, commercial access to vehicle identity, repair information, labour times, diagnostics, illustrated parts, pricing, availability and ordering for Asia-Pacific, Europe/UK and North America.

365 is a Philippines-first, multi-tenant vehicle marketplace and Shop Manager platform planning regional expansion. Its repair products are sold as four separate modules:

- Automotive Repair;
- Motorcycle & Powersports;
- Heavy Truck & Fleet;
- Marine & Watercraft.

Each provider request must name one or more modules explicitly. Rights and prices are not assumed to transfer from Automotive to Motorcycle, Heavy Truck or Marine.


### 1.1 Complete before sending outreach

Fill and approve these sender facts once; then use them consistently in every form, email, NDA and contract:

- legal contracting entity and incorporation jurisdiction;
- registered address and billing/tax details;
- sender's full name, title, company email and phone;
- commercial decision owner, technical owner and legal/privacy owner;
- exact first-pilot country, module and intended user type;
- realistic pilot shops, seats, assets, monthly lookups and orders as ranges—not promises;
- desired sandbox start and target production window;
- data hosting regions and current security/privacy documentation;
- whether 365 will contract directly, through a regional entity or through a local distributor;
- approved one-page company overview and architecture/security summary.

Until these are confirmed, use the messages as drafts and request only an exploratory call, coverage information and pilot requirements. Do not sign an NDA, order form, reseller agreement or minimum-volume commitment under an entity name that has not been approved.

## 2. Short company description for vendor forms

365 Motor Sales is building a Philippines-first automotive and mobility marketplace with a multi-tenant Shop Manager for independent repair businesses, fleets and specialist service providers. Shop Manager already supports customers, assets, work orders, inspections, labour, parts, technician time, quotes, invoices, payments, inventory, reminders and service history.

We are developing four independently purchasable technical modules—Automotive, Motorcycle & Powersports, Heavy Truck & Fleet, and Marine & Watercraft—and are seeking licensed data and integration partners for Asia-Pacific launch coverage followed by Europe/UK and North America.

We are interested in structured APIs, licensed data feeds, embedded viewers, approved deep links, diagnostic report integrations and OEM/distributor business-partner services. We do not intend to scrape, mirror or redistribute provider content outside written contract rights.

## 9. Request-for-information questionnaire

### A. Company and product

1. What legal entity supplies and licences the product?
2. What product/module names and versions are proposed?
3. Is the offering an API, feed, viewer, deep link, portal, SDK, device/report bridge or business-partner API?
4. Which of the four 365 repair modules may use it?
5. Which countries, languages and currencies are contractually supported?
6. Which OEMs or third parties own the underlying content?

### B. Coverage

7. Can you provide a machine-readable coverage file?
8. What identity keys are supported: VIN, chassis, frame, model code, HIN, engine serial, component serial or registration?
9. Is market/territory part of the identity?
10. How are mid-year changes, option codes, engine/transmission combinations and multi-stage vehicles handled?
11. How are trucks with replaced components and boats with repowered engines handled?
12. Does unsupported mean an explicit no-match, or does the product return nearest-family results?
13. What are the typical publication delays for new models?
14. What historical model years are covered?
15. Which EV, hybrid, high-voltage, hydrogen and ADAS capabilities are included?
16. Provide exact coverage results against the attached 365 test file.

### C. Data capabilities

17. Which labour operations and overlap rules are available?
18. Are labour values OEM warranty, standard, aftermarket benchmark or provider-derived?
19. Which procedures, specifications, torque sequences, wiring diagrams and component locations are included?
20. Which DTC definitions, guided tests, scan functions and programming procedures are included?
21. Which maintenance schedules and severe-duty variants are included?
22. Are illustrated parts, supersessions, quantities and assembly relationships included?
23. Are price, tax, stock, lead time, order, cancellation, return, core and warranty services included?
24. Are recalls, campaigns, bulletins and completion status available?
25. How are corrections, supersessions and withdrawn content delivered?

### D. Integration

26. Is a sandbox available and does it use production-like data?
27. What authentication method is used?
28. What are the rate, batch, concurrency and asset limits?
29. What uptime and latency targets are offered?
30. Which environments, SDKs, schemas and API versions are supported?
31. How are breaking changes and deprecations communicated?
32. Are webhooks or delta feeds available?
33. What caching is required or permitted?
34. Is offline or low-bandwidth access supported?
35. What observability, request IDs, usage reports and support escalation are available?
36. Can a provider vehicle/asset ID be retained after contract termination?

### E. Rights and restrictions

37. Is multi-tenant SaaS use permitted?
38. May independent shops, fleets, technicians, customers and public marketplace users see different subsets?
39. May data appear in estimates, work orders, inspections, invoices, emails or PDFs?
40. May diagrams or documents be printed?
41. May 365 index the content for search?
42. May content be translated or summarised?
43. May provider content be sent to an AI model for retrieval, classification or answer generation?
44. May 365 store derived repair outcomes, shop notes and aggregated benchmarks?
45. What attribution and branding are required?
46. What territory and geofencing rules apply?
47. What retention, deletion and backup rules apply?
48. What access logs or audits must 365 provide?
49. What subcontractor, hosting and cross-border data restrictions apply?

### F. Security and privacy

50. Which security certifications and penetration-test evidence are available?
51. How are credentials, key rotation and production access managed?
52. What breach-notification and incident-response terms apply?
53. Is personal data processed, and under which roles/agreements?
54. Which secure-gateway, programming, immobiliser or SERMI/OEM controls apply?
55. Can high-risk functions be disabled by module, shop, user and vehicle?

### G. Commercials

56. What pilot, implementation, onboarding and training fees apply?
57. Is production pricing per module, region, shop, location, seat, asset, call, order or revenue?
58. Are there minimum commitments or prepaid volumes?
59. What overage, currency, tax and annual-increase terms apply?
60. Can 365 receive early-stage/startup and Philippines pilot pricing?
61. May 365 resell access as a separately identified provider data pass?
62. Are marketing approval or reference-customer obligations included?
63. What support hours, languages and service levels are included?
64. What renewal, suspension and termination terms apply?
65. What wind-down period and deletion/export evidence are available?

## 10. Pilot proposal

- **Duration:** 30–45 days after sandbox credentials and documentation.
- **Scope:** one module, one region and one clearly named capability set.
- **Test data:** at least 25 exact local assets; increase to 100 before production commitment.
- **Users:** restricted internal evaluators and named pilot shops only where authorised.
- **Integration:** server-side proof of concept; no public launch.
- **Success:** exact identity, contracted capability coverage, no silent substitutions, rights confirmation, acceptable latency/support and sustainable unit economics.
- **Deliverables:** scored coverage CSV, API/error report, legal-rights matrix, pricing model, risk list and go/no-go recommendation.
- **Production:** separate written approval and contract; successful sandbox testing alone does not authorise customer use.

## 13. Internal outreach rules

- Tailor each message to one provider product and primary module.
- Do not send confidential customer VINs, HINs or serials until an appropriate agreement and secure transfer method exist; use consented or test identifiers.
- Do not place passwords, tokens, API keys, private price sheets, NDAs or contracts in GitHub.
- Record verbal promises as open questions until confirmed in writing.
- Never describe portal access as an API or redistribution licence.
- Never promise user counts, order volumes or launch dates that are not approved.
- Do not let a provider bundle force Automotive to become a prerequisite for Motorcycle, Heavy Truck or Marine.
- Keep all technical trials non-production until legal rights and security gates pass.
