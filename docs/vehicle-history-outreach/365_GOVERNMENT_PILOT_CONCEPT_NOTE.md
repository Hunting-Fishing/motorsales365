## 1. Proposal

365 Motor Sales proposes a non-exclusive, privacy-minimized pilot connecting vehicle identity and authorized status verification with consented repair, inspection, odometer, installed-part and diagnostic events generated through participating Philippine repair shops.

The pilot is intended to test whether better source-labelled vehicle lifecycle information can improve road safety, consumer protection, transfer readiness, component traceability and data quality without exposing vehicle-owner personal information.

## 2. Public-service outcomes

- Reduce VIN, chassis, frame, engine and plate mismatches.
- Help buyers identify when official review is required before payment or transfer.
- Encourage timely and correct transfer of ownership.
- Identify odometer inconsistencies through repeated verified observations.
- Improve disclosure of total loss, material damage and restoration status when authorized data is available.
- Document major engine, chassis, frame and regulated component changes.
- Increase recall and safety-campaign completion.
- Preserve installed-part and repair evidence for warranty, claims and roadworthiness.
- Produce de-identified fleet-quality insights if specifically approved.

## 3. Data 365 can contribute

Participating shops can generate:

- identifier observations and prescribed photographs;
- odometer/engine-hour readings with evidence;
- repair-order date and public-safe operation summaries;
- independent inspections;
- exact parts purchased, installed, removed and warrantied;
- technician-confirmed installation and post-repair verification;
- diagnostic pre-scan/post-scan summaries linked to the repair order;
- recall/campaign completion evidence where lawfully confirmed; and
- correction, dispute and source-quality information.

A purchased part is never represented as installed without an installation event.

## 4. Initial government request

365 is not requesting:

- a copy of the LTO registry;
- unrestricted bulk access;
- owner names, addresses, telephone numbers, email, government IDs or signatures;
- full OR/CR images;
- investigative details; or
- permission to resell raw government records.

365 requests discussion of a controlled status-verification capability. A submitted identity could receive only approved non-personal responses such as exact match, mismatch, unable to verify, active/inactive/unknown status, process/review required, approved vehicle descriptors, source timestamp and official response reference.

LTO or partner-controlled middleware would remain authoritative. Every request would be authenticated, purpose-bound, rate-limited and logged. Consumer display would be limited to fields expressly approved in writing.

## 5. Proposed pilot phases

### Phase A - 365-owned event capture

- No government data.
- Shops capture identity, mileage, service, installed-part, inspection and scan events.
- Synthetic sample reports and data-quality baseline.

### Phase B - sandbox

- Synthetic or masked partner test data.
- Identity matching, minimal-field response, security, correction and audit testing.
- No consumer release.

### Phase C - allowlisted live verification

- Approved shops, users and purposes only.
- Daily volume and report limits.
- Minimal authorized fields.
- Joint monitoring and rapid suspension.

### Phase D - evaluation

- Joint privacy, security, accuracy, public-service and consumer-fairness report.
- Written decision to stop, remediate, extend or expand.

## 6. Safeguards

- Data minimization and separate private, partner-restricted and public-report zones.
- DPO oversight, Privacy Impact Assessment and privacy management program.
- Role, tenant, purpose, field, jurisdiction and source-based authorization.
- MFA for privileged users; managed credentials and key rotation.
- Encryption, signed requests, replay protection and immutable audit records.
- Anti-enumeration, rate limits and bulk-export controls.
- No previous-owner personal information in consumer reports.
- Append-only evidence/correction trail with lawful deletion, blocking and pseudonymization when required.
- Formal dispute, urgent suppression and source-correction workflow.
- Incident response capable of applicable Philippine notification deadlines.
- Independent security testing before live data.

## 7. Pilot success measures

- No unauthorized disclosure of previous-owner data.
- No automatic merge when critical identifiers conflict.
- At least 99.5% exact attachment of tested events to the intended pilot asset; all exceptions reviewed.
- Every report states sources, country/market, search time, coverage and limitations.
- Every installed-part event links to an RO and technician confirmation.
- Every partner query records user, purpose, authorization, source and outcome.
- Demonstrated buyer understanding that no record is not proof of no event.
- Measurable improvement in identity, disclosure, inspection and transfer readiness.
