# 365 Verified Vehicle History - Buyer and Seller Safety Playbook

## 1. Safety promise

365 helps people make a better-informed decision. It does not declare a vehicle safe solely because a report contains no negative event. Every buyer is guided toward physical identity checks, document verification, a current inspection, a test drive and official transfer procedures.

## 2. Report anatomy

Every report should include:

1. Masked identity and exact match/conflict status.
2. Source and country coverage table with search times.
3. Mileage/usage timeline and inconsistency indicators.
4. Damage, insurance and total-loss events when authorized.
5. Official and independent inspections shown separately.
6. Service/repair timeline with public-safe summaries.
7. Installed part and major component lifecycle.
8. Diagnostic scan summary and unresolved/new faults where permitted.
9. Recall/campaign status.
10. Auction, import/export, listing and custody events where licensed.
11. Disputed/corrected/superseded events.
12. Limitations and recommended next checks.

## 3. Evidence labels

Use words, icons and explanatory tooltips - not color alone.

- Government verified.
- Law-enforcement verification/referral.
- Insurer reported.
- Inspection-centre verified.
- OEM/dealer reported.
- 365 verified shop event.
- Diagnostic-device captured.
- Technician-confirmed installation.
- Owner submitted with reviewed evidence.
- Owner submitted, unverified.
- Inferred risk indicator, not proof.
- Disputed/pending review.
- Corrected/superseded.

## 4. Buyer workflow

### Before viewing

- Compare listing VIN/chassis/frame/plate photos with the report preview.
- Check available jurisdictions and sources before buying the report.
- Avoid sellers who will not permit a physical identifier and document check.

### At the vehicle

- Compare identifiers on body/frame, dashboard, engine and documents; photograph only with seller permission.
- Look for altered, re-stamped, damaged or mismatched identifier areas.
- Compare physical odometer and wear with history.
- Confirm seller identity/authority without uploading their unredacted ID into chat.
- Request a current 365 pre-purchase inspection and appropriate diagnostic scan.

### Before payment

- Review material findings and open disputes.
- Verify official transfer, HPG clearance and other current LTO requirements directly through approved procedures.
- Use a written sale agreement and safe payment trail.
- If escrow is offered, it must be through a properly licensed/contracted provider; 365 must not hold regulated funds without authority.
- Do not rely on screenshots; validate the report ID/QR and issue time.

### After purchase

- Complete transfer promptly.
- Claim the vehicle profile through a safe custody process.
- Revoke seller/buyer temporary shares when no longer needed.
- Store the bill of sale, transfer documents, inspection and report snapshot.

## 5. Seller workflow

### Prepare

- Claim the profile using the least intrusive acceptable evidence.
- Verify current identifiers and correct inaccurate history early.
- Upload service evidence into the private vault.
- Complete known safety repairs and recall work.
- Obtain a fresh inspection and report.

### Disclose

- Disclose known material damage, flood, total-loss/restoration, odometer, engine/chassis change and unresolved safety issues.
- Share eligible documents through time-limited links with redaction.
- Do not post OR/CR, IDs, addresses or signatures publicly.
- Do not describe `no record` as `never damaged` or `guaranteed clean`.

### Sell and transfer

- Meet in a safe location and retain transaction evidence.
- Use a clear sale agreement.
- Follow current LTO/HPG transfer requirements.
- Transfer profile custody without transferring previous-owner PII.
- Revoke buyer/seller links after completion.

## 6. Owner vehicle claim levels

- `shop_witnessed` - identity verified during an in-person service event with customer authorization.
- `document_reviewed` - current custody/ownership evidence reviewed under a minimized process.
- `partner_verified` - authoritative partner confirms the relationship when available.
- `unverified_claim` - private staging only; cannot alter or publish history.

An owner claim never gives permission to delete accurate source events.

## 7. Shop safety workflow

### Check-in

- Use existing asset when identifiers match.
- Capture prescribed identifier and odometer evidence.
- Create a conflict instead of merging near matches.
- Record consent/notice and selected RO.

### Repair

- Keep complaint, diagnosis, costs and internal notes private.
- Separate quoted, ordered, received, allocated and installed parts.
- Capture stronger evidence for critical components.
- Run post-repair verification appropriate to the work.

### Close-out

- Show the customer the public-safe events that will be published.
- Publish through a signed event batch.
- Provide private repair packet and correction route.

## 8. High-risk findings and language

| Finding | Public-safe language |
|---|---|
| Lower later mileage | Mileage inconsistency detected; verify odometer and source records |
| Identifier mismatch | Vehicle identity requires review; do not complete payment/transfer until resolved |
| Theft/alarm response | Official verification required; follow authorized agency instructions |
| Total-loss source event | Source reported a total-loss/restoration category; review source, repair and inspection evidence |
| Flood indicator only | Possible flood-related indicator; not proof by itself; inspect vehicle and supporting evidence |
| No event returned | No reportable event was returned by this source at the stated time; unreported events may exist |
| Disputed event | Event is under review; use the dispute details and supporting checks before deciding |

Do not publish blame, criminal conclusions or mechanical-safety certification without proper authority.

## 9. Report freshness

- Show report issue time and last source refresh.
- Show unavailable/timed-out sources separately.
- Expire official status labels according to the source agreement.
- Encourage a new status check immediately before transaction.
- Keep the purchased snapshot and identify later corrections.

## 10. Anti-stalking and privacy

- Hide previous-owner identity.
- Generalize service location and recent event time on public reports.
- Do not show a live or recent location trail.
- Require purpose and authorization for sensitive searches.
- Limit reports per account/device/payment instrument.
- Watermark reports and investigate unusual access.

## 11. Dispute experience

The report provides a visible `Report an error` action. The claimant selects the exact event/field, explains the concern and uploads only necessary evidence. Urgent wrong-vehicle or identity conflicts receive immediate suppression or prominent review status. The final decision explains whether the event was confirmed, corrected, superseded, removed from public display or remains disputed.

## 12. Buyer/seller checklist

### Buyer

- [ ] Physical identifiers match listing, report and documents.
- [ ] Report QR/hash validates.
- [ ] Coverage and unavailable sources reviewed.
- [ ] Mileage timeline reviewed.
- [ ] Current inspection and test drive completed.
- [ ] Diagnostic scan appropriate to vehicle completed.
- [ ] Seller authority and transfer requirements verified.
- [ ] Written agreement and safe payment trail prepared.

### Seller

- [ ] Vehicle profile claimed and identifiers reviewed.
- [ ] Material facts disclosed.
- [ ] Incorrect records disputed before listing.
- [ ] Fresh inspection/report attached.
- [ ] Private documents redacted and shared by expiring link.
- [ ] Transfer steps and profile handoff prepared.
