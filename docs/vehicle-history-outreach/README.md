# 365 Vehicle History Network - Complete Outreach Package

Prepared for 365 Motor Sales Philippines on 2026-08-06.

This folder is the controlled source package for approaching Philippine government agencies, insurers, inspection providers, repair shops, OEM/dealers, parts suppliers, diagnostic providers, lenders, auctions, importers and exporters.

- `365_PRE_OUTREACH_READINESS_CHECKLIST.csv` - send/no-send gate.

## Use rules

1. Complete every `BLOCKER` item in the readiness checklist before contacting LTO or requesting live government data.
2. Replace every bracketed placeholder and have Philippine counsel plus the DPO review the outgoing version.
3. Send the six-page concept note first. Do not attach the 59-page master playbook to a cold email. Keep the security assurance and RFI available for technical/legal follow-up.
4. Do not say that LTO, PNP-HPG, the Insurance Commission, an insurer or another source has endorsed or integrated with 365 until a signed agreement and tested production connection exist.
5. Do not request a registry copy, owner names, addresses, IDs or unrestricted bulk access. Request a controlled status-verification pilot with approved non-personal fields.
6. Launch the 365-owned shop/parts/inspection/scan history MVP independently of government approval.
7. Treat every report as source-limited. A no-record result is not a clean-history guarantee.
